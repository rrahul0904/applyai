# Product Vision

## Product promise

ApplyAI helps a candidate move from career intent to a better job outcome:

DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → PREPARE → APPLY → TRACK → FOLLOW UP →
INTERVIEW → NEGOTIATE → LEARN

The product is a structured career platform with AI embedded in bounded
workflows. It is not a chatbot wrapped around a job board.

## Candidate outcome

The north star is better, higher-intent applications and improved outcomes—not
application volume. A candidate should understand:

- which opportunities fit their goals;
- why a job is relevant;
- which facts came from their documents and which they verified;
- what was submitted to each employer;
- what changed in every application;
- what is working across their search.

## Principles

1. Candidate data is private by default.
2. Canonical product data precedes AI generation.
3. Job freshness, provenance, and deduplication are first-class.
4. Match explanations must be traceable to structured inputs.
5. The user approves submissions and factual profile changes.
6. Fewer, better applications are preferable to indiscriminate automation.

## Delivery horizon

- **CURRENT:** Candidate foundation, authenticated ownership, canonical job
  model, and migration/test discipline.
- **PLANNED:** First complete candidate vertical slice and one legitimate job
  source.
- **FUTURE SCALE:** Explainable matching, AI preparation tools, native mobile,
  employer workflows, and high-volume search.
