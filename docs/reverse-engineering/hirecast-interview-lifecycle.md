# Hirecast-style interview lifecycle

## ApplyAI Fit

**APPLYAI — PREPARE / FULL.** The qualifying behavior directly strengthens interview preparation, practice, readiness measurement, and career evidence reuse.

## Why this qualifies

The useful public behavior is a job-specific preparation lifecycle rather than a generic content site: company/role context, staged interview rounds, practice prompts, flashcards, notes, retrospectives, readiness, interview stories, and audio-friendly briefing content all sit inside ApplyAI's prepare-to-interview journey.

## Candidate journey stages

- prepare for interviews
- practice/mock
- measure readiness
- manage career intelligence

## Absorb into ApplyAI

- four-phase interview lifecycle: recruiter, hiring manager, technical/case, executive/culture
- company, role, resume and Career Memory grounding
- quizzes, flashcards, cheat sheets and private notes
- current-phase-only retrospectives with carry-forward actions
- readiness evidence across phases and practice attempts
- reusable STAR story bank tied to verified career evidence
- five short podcast-style briefing scripts with browser playback; hosted audio is optional rather than fabricated

## Keep separate

- copied proprietary prompts, private source material or hidden APIs
- unverified interviewer-specific claims
- an RSS/audio feed when no real audio asset exists
- any behavior that bypasses ApplyAI's existing evidence and privacy boundaries

## Implementation destination

Extend the existing ApplyAI Prepare job workspace and backend. Reuse current mock interview, recordings, skill-gap, learning-path and readiness infrastructure rather than creating a second preparation product.
